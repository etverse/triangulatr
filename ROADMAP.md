# triangulatr roadmap

triangulatr will collect results from the other etverse packages through `tidy()` and `glance()` and assess whether they agree. The [etverse design](https://etverse.github.io/design.html) fixes its scope and interface. Its methods are chosen from the literature before the first code, and each planned item will be a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/etverse/triangulatr/issues).

## Not planned here

- Estimation and bias analysis: the packages whose results it collects.
